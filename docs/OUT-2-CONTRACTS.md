Missing, malformed, unsupported, expired or disabled permission means manual approval only, never allow. A permission must have a bounded expiry; proposed pilot maximum 30 days, subject to AUTO review. First five eligible recipients (or all if fewer) require individual version-bound approvals before enabling; no blanket first-batch button grants automation. Standing sequences remain OUT-11, after manual pilot OUT-9. Bespoke replies cannot inherit this grant.

Only an authenticated authorised owner can grant/edit/revoke through AUTO’s settings boundary. Model/tool arguments cannot supply authority or owner identity. Lock the settings row and merge only the named kind with revision compare-and-swap; never overwrite invoice-chase settings. Campaign revision changes invalidate queued automatic work. Mailbox, brand, target, template or allowed-variable changes require a new first-batch review and fresh permission. Lower limits apply immediately; raising them cannot exceed pilot ceilings silently. Pause-all and revocation are effective for all unclaimed work. Revocation and final dispatch claim serialize on the same authority lock; a claim committed first may already be in flight and cannot be recalled. State that boundary in the UI and audit.

### Immutable action and execution boundary

Payload binds business context, campaign/version, prospect, enrolment/step, sequence/template version, mailbox, provider, From, To/CC, subject/body/sign-off/footer, knowledge references, reply message/thread references, send window, expiry and logical idempotency key. Hash canonical payload; version is mandatory on outreach approvals (human and automatic). Approval never silently regenerates copy or changes sender.

Automatic proposals bind a typed authority reference `{kind, settings_revision, campaign_id, campaign_revision}` plus immutable grant snapshot. AUTO must resolve this from trusted settings, not accept a supplied snapshot as proof. Audit records the grant owner and timestamp separately from `approved_by`; automatic `approved_by` remains NULL, human approval remains a UUID. Proposed shared action metadata: `authority_type` human/automation, `automation_kind`, `authority_revision`, `authority_snapshot`. Exact columns versus schema-versioned metadata are AUTO-owned RED migration decisions; only one implementation across departments.

Execution sequence:

1. Select a due logical job, scoped by business; perform complete mailbox sync through AUTO-2. Proposed freshness maximum: 60 seconds, including every relevant page/event; incomplete sync is not fresh.
2. In one transaction lock settings/authority, mailbox quota, tenant quota and prospect/enrolment in a consistent shared order. Recheck permission/card version, entitlement, campaign state, eligibility/LIA/notice, suppression, any reply, owner takeover, one-answer slot, mailbox ownership/health, verification freshness, time window and caps.
3. Atomically reserve usage and, for a bespoke answer, the prospect-wide answer slot; claim the action with a unique logical operation key and durable dispatch marker. No network send before commit. AUTO’s present claim transaction needs an outreach hook for these atomic reservations; separate commits are not acceptable.
4. Call the mailbox-bound adapter once. Persist accepted/definitely-not-accepted/unknown with provider evidence, then settle the reservation atomically with idempotent metering. Failure to persist after sending is unknown. Do not re-execute a claimed action after lease expiry, crash or timeout.

Proposed internal contract names for discussion: `reserve_outreach_usage(business, operation_key, mailbox, prospect, period)` and `settle_outreach_usage(operation_key, outcome, evidence)`. They are not present APIs. Reserve locks enforce business month + business day + mailbox day + contact spacing together. The action claim and reservation must share the service transaction. A reservation holds capacity until definite rejection or reconciliation; unknown outcomes never release automatically. Confirmed acceptance without a message ID still counts once. Outbox is a projection, not the dispatch authority.

`outreach_prospects_researched` counts one completed evidence bundle, including no-fit; retries of a job do not count again. `outreach_emails_sent` counts accepted initial/follow-up/bespoke messages once. Business allowances are 200/500 monthly across mailboxes/brands, no overage. Provider credits are separate. UK calendar periods are recorded at reservation; an unclaimed reservation is released/rebooked if its period changes before dispatch; an already dispatched operation settles its original period. Unknowns retain their original hold and block that contact. Founder exemption requires verified entitlement, not business-name matching.

## 3. Mailbox contract

`backend/models.py:291` defines `email_accounts` with business, provider, address, display name and `is_default`; there is no model-level one-account-per-business constraint. Public account listing excludes ciphertext at `backend/app/email/router.py:311`. Default selection is currently two updates at `:324`, so concurrent default changes need a transaction/unique constraint before relying on a single default. Google callback stores a business/account identity at `:1540`; Microsoft at `:1614`. End-to-end second-account connection and reconnect identity preservation are acceptance tests, not proven by these writes.

Each campaign requires a tenant-owned `email_accounts.id`, selected explicitly and shown on every card. Brand default is a creation-time convenience copied into a draft campaign, never a dynamic send-time lookup. Several campaigns may share an inbox; FitFutures and New Body may use different inboxes on the same domain. Changing a default or connecting a newer account cannot change an existing campaign. Once any message is dispatched, moving to another mailbox requires a new campaign; old history/replies remain on the original inbox. Disconnect/re-auth failure pauses its campaigns; no fallback to another inbox. Reconnect must preserve identity or require explicit remapping/review. SMTP is outside the pilot until equivalent thread/sync/header guarantees are demonstrated.

### Transport and matching

Proposed adapter input: scoped account object, immutable message operation ID, To/CC, subject/text/HTML, RFC Message-ID, In-Reply-To/References, provider reply target/thread, and allowlisted unsubscribe headers. Never accept arbitrary headers, From spoofing, model-chosen credentials or business IDs. Output: `outcome`, `mailbox_id`, `provider`, optional `provider_message_id`, `provider_thread_id`, `rfc_message_id`, `accepted_at`, safe error code and reconciliation reference. Provider acceptance is not delivery.

`backend/providers/base.py:11` currently returns only an optional message ID. Gmail sends raw MIME without explicit threadId at `backend/providers/google_gmail.py:68` and returns only ID at `:76`; Graph uses sendMail at `backend/providers/microsoft_graph.py:85`, attaches inReplyTo at `:97` and returns no ID at `:107`. Neither current send signature accepts CC/References/custom headers. OUT-6 must extend transport contracts and test Gmail threadId + RFC headers and Graph’s genuine reply/draft workflow. Exact provider behaviour/header preservation is **UNVERIFIED** offline; no inference that current Graph inReplyTo works. A known 202 acceptance with missing ID must not trigger resend; keep thread-dependent follow-ups blocked until correlated.

Inbound event contract from AUTO-2: business, mailbox, provider message/thread ID, RFC references, sender/recipients, provider/received/ingested times, body availability, completeness and sync watermark. `backend/models.py:337` already stores business/account/message/thread; `:369` starts per-account sync state. Gmail current sync reads the latest 50 INBOX IDs (`backend/providers/google_gmail.py:88`) and can skip fetch failures (`:109`); that is insufficient proof of a complete freshness watermark. AUTO-2 must address pagination, missed events and body retrieval; do not substitute a message-count sweep.

Deduplicate by business + mailbox + provider message ID. Match by that same business/mailbox + thread, checked against known participants and RFC references. Never join thread IDs across inboxes, match by subject alone, or trust an ambiguous sender. Ambiguity pauses possible affected enrolments for review. Any genuine inbound match pauses immediately before classification, invalidates queued sequence authority, and applies tenant-wide prospect suppression on objection. Ignore self-sent events; OOO pauses but is not a human reply. Preserve timestamps to explain the unavoidable remote reply/final-check race. Replies UI filters by mailbox; the one bespoke answer limit is per prospect across all inboxes/campaigns.

### Mailbox operational limits

Mailbox state is keyed `(business_id, mailbox_id)`, never domain alone: enabled/paused, warm-up stage, counted sending days, cap, health evidence/time, sync freshness and failure reason. Domain evidence may be reused as evidence, but each From identity needs alignment verification and its own operational verdict.

Apply 5/day for five sending days, then 10/day for five, then 20/day only after owner health review. A sending day is a Europe/London date with accepted outreach; an unknown holds capacity but does not advance warm-up. Initial/follow-up/bespoke sends share the mailbox cap across campaigns; tenant cap stays 20/day across every inbox. Reconnect, new campaign or new month never resets warm-up upward or resets daily usage. Provider limits and lower owner caps win. Windows are weekdays 09:00 inclusive to 17:00 exclusive UK; sequence delays 5 then 7 business days from accepted predecessor, with the later seven-calendar-day minimum spacing across campaigns for that prospect. No downtime catch-up burst.

Verify domain control, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment using DNS and authorised signed-message evidence before activation and daily. Unknown/stale/failed health blocks sends. Hard bounce suppresses recipient; complaint pauses mailbox immediately; three hard bounces in last 50 accepted mailbox sends triggers review. No synthetic warm-up or mailbox rotation to evade limits. Unsubscribe HTTPS POST is idempotent and unauthenticated via opaque scoped token; GET confirms without mutation. OUT-6 must prove header signing/preservation and token isolation. Pausing/cancellation never disables inbound objections.

### Secrets, quotas and failures

Use the Fernet pattern in `backend/email_utils.py:8`–`:27` (missing key and invalid ciphertext raise); OAuth encrypts tokens via `backend/providers/oauth_utils.py:47`. Proposed dedicated backend-only integration record stores `api_key_ciphertext`, `key_version`, provider, enabled/status, saved/tested/revoked timestamps and actor. Encrypt a structured value containing business/integration/provider identity and validate that binding on decrypt to resist ciphertext substitution. Encryption material is deployment-managed, never stored with ciphertext or in business settings. No plaintext fallback. Key replacement is write-only; Settings shows connected/status, never returns key or ciphertext. Only owner-authorised backend operations can save/test/disconnect. Disconnect cancels future calls; in-flight calls may finish and are audited. Rotation re-encrypts with a recorded version under a separately reviewed procedure.

Counts-only operational logs: provider/operation/status class, call count, latency and quota debit estimate/actual; no keys, request person/address/domain, result body or URL query. Minimized prospect provenance belongs in scoped evidence, not logs. Safe Aria projections expose connection health and usage, never credentials. Separate Settings display: provider-reported remaining credits with observation time (or “unknown”) and locally reserved/observed calls; local counts are not asserted to equal billable credits. Other uses of the customer’s provider account can consume quota outside Business Hero.

Reserve an owner-configured daily request/credit ceiling before each call; all concurrent jobs for the integration share it. If documented credit cost is unknown, paid operation stays disabled pending verification/consent. Preflight suppression/eligibility before enrichment and recheck before saving a result; an objection arriving mid-call cancels profiling and discards unnecessary returned personal data. Cache only permitted, tenant-local, unexpired evidence; a cache hit is not a new provider debit. Provider credits and BH completed-prospect allowance are separately reserved/accounted.

401/403: mark credentials/plan access invalid, pause provider jobs, ask owner to reconnect/check plan; no repeated automatic attempts. 400/422: permanent validation failure, no retry. Not-found/no-match: completed no-contact evidence, no guessed email; actual provider charging follows verified contract. 429: respect documented Retry-After, bounded jitter/backoff and integration circuit breaker, no provider hopping. Quota exhausted: pause until observed reset or owner intervention. Timeout/5xx: outcome/credit use may be unknown; hold budget and avoid automatic paid replay unless documentation proves safe or owner authorises a fresh charged attempt. Malformed/oversized response or unrecognized verdict: discard/flag, block send. Persist sanitized error codes, never provider exception bodies that may echo secrets.
