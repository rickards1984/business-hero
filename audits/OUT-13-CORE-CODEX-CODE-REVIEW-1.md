2. **P1 — Housekeeping can consume the entire new-candidate budget.** [outreach_erasure.py:495](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-OUT-13/backend/services/outreach_erasure.py:495) starts the 120-second timer before pruning, reconciling and summary scrubbing. Summary scrubbing is also unbounded across businesses. **Fix:** independently bound housekeeping and preserve the promised new-candidate budget; test housekeeping exceeding 120 seconds while an overdue candidate still receives processing.

Cover cards, prospect/evidence reads and stage lists.

ApprovalCard and StageDialog also lack a deadline-triggered render. **Fix:** introduce a reactive deadline clock and recompute withholding independently of query success. Test an idle open screen crossing the deadline during failed refreshes, as T9 requires.
