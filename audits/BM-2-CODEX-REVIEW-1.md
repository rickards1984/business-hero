1. **P1 — Concurrent sessions can spend the same remaining cap.** [backend/realtime_voice.py:881](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/backend/realtime_voice.py:881). Admission calculates a fixed duration from accrued usage without reserving allowance or overage budget. Heartbeats do not consume that budget. Reproduced with exhausted board allowance and a £9 cap: two overlapping sessions each receive 1,200 seconds and accrue **£18 total**. Cross-pool overlap has the same problem. Admission needs shared budget coordination; summing completed overage is insufficient.

2. **P2 — Board-triggered spend-cap notices are silently discarded.** [backend/services/metering.py:317](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/backend/services/metering.py:317). Board accrual queues the shared cap notice, but delivery validates it against default `usage()`—the voice pool. When receptionist minutes remain, that pool’s state is `ok` despite the exhausted shared cap. Reproduced: board state `spend_cap_reached`, voice state `ok`, pending notice deleted, **zero notifications delivered**. Validate shared-cap notices against shared spend independently of pool allowance.

3. **P2 — Ending a meeting does not stop voice before extraction.** [MeetingChat.tsx:390](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/frontend/client/src/components/board-meeting/MeetingChat.tsx:390). The voice component remains mounted while `endMeeting()` runs; it unmounts only after the response. Extraction snapshots the transcript while voice continues producing—and billing—turns. A commitment spoken during this interval can appear in messages but never enter the summary or extracted actions. Stop voice and establish transcript persistence before calling `/end`.

4. **P2 — A spoken turn can be inserted after the meeting finishes.** [executive_meeting_orchestrator.py:1068](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/backend/services/executive_meeting_orchestrator.py:1068). The status check and unconditional INSERT are separate operations without synchronization with meeting completion. A writer can read `in_progress`, another request complete the meeting, and the writer then insert into the finished meeting. Make the write/status transition mutually consistent and test this interleaving.

5. **P2 — Bounded writer cleanup permanently drops acknowledged turns.** [backend/realtime_voice.py:1230](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/backend/realtime_voice.py:1230). After the drain timeout, cancelling the writer abandons queued turns without durable recovery. A scaled-time probe delivered two transcripts to the browser, delayed the first database write beyond the drain bound, and persisted **only the first**. Ordinary write exceptions also discard turns. Preserve bounded socket teardown while making accepted turns recoverable.

6. **P2 — “Finish speaking” tracks generation, not audible playback.** [backend/realtime_voice.py:964](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/backend/realtime_voice.py:964), [RealtimeVoice.tsx:272](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/frontend/client/src/components/RealtimeVoice.tsx:272). `response.done` clears `responding`, but the browser may still have queued audio. 7. **P2 — The 80% warning never updates during a voice session.** [MeetingChat.tsx:93](/Users/michaelrickards/dev/bh2-BM-2/frontend/client/src/components/board-meeting/MeetingChat.tsx:93).
