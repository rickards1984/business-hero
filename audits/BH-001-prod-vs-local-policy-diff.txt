WHAT THIS LICENSES
  BH-003's RLS suite runs against the local replay. Because the replay's
  policy and RLS state is byte-identical to production's, a green run there
  now supports a statement about production's POLICIES, not merely about the
  migration files — which is the gap test_tenant_isolation_rls_path.py's own
  docstring and UNCOVERED registry flagged, and the reason this comparison
  was the first thing done with the CSVs.

WHAT IT DOES NOT LICENSE
  * Grants beyond `businesses` — the Q3 export is truncated at 100 rows.
  * Column-level grants — the Q4 export is truncated and never reached
    `businesses`. This is what RC1 P0-3 turns on.
  * Constraints — no export covers pg_constraint.
  * VIEWS — a view cannot carry RLS and Q1 filters relkind='r'. Production
    has two (receptionist_call_stats, support_stats) that the replay does not
    create; see BH-001-FINDINGS.md §5. The replay is NOT equivalent there.
  * The `auth` and `storage` schemas.
